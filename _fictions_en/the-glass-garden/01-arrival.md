The train had been climbing for two hours before anyone noticed the light was
wrong. It came in low through the left-hand windows, the colour of weak tea,
and it did not move the way afternoon light moves.

At the fourth stop, which had no name on the timetable, a woman got off and did
not look back at the carriage. That was how I learned the stop existed.
