The last post was my review of 2022. As always, here are a few challenging
targets for 2023. There are also things in life that simply have to be faced;
those do not go on the list.

1. Reading (15–50 books)
2. Broadening the view (6 new cities, and write the travel notes)
3. Certificates (driving licence, professional exams)
4. Interests (6–20 paintings, skateboarding)
5. Health (BMI into the normal range; every weight-related condition gone)
6. Writing (5–10 papers, 50 articles, publish a collection of drawings)

——

Paper of the day: [REVIEW: Proteasome System of Protein Degradation and
Processing][paper] — on the ubiquitin–proteasome system: proteasome structure,
the ubiquitination machinery, the classical ATP/Ub-dependent mechanism of
protein degradation, alternative mechanisms of proteasomal degradation and
processing, and the damage to the proteasome system that leads to various
diseases.
